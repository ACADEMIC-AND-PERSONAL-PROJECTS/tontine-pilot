# TontinePilot

Community AI copilot for rotating savings groups (tontines). Tracks contributions, smart reminders, anomaly detection, exportable ledger. AWS Builder Hackathon · #workplace-efficiency · #community.

**Audience:** English-speaking hackathon judges + Francophone Senegalese community groups.
**Mode:** Landing = Persuade · App = Operate.
**Constraint:** Never moves real money — declaration & tracking only.
